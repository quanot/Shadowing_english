Set up your practice

1. Load a video (or audio) file.

2. Load its subtitle file (.srt / .vtt) — Whisper-generated subtitles work best.

3. Listen to a sentence, press Speak, repeat it, and get scored word by word.

4. 💾 Save to library keeps the video and your progress on this device.

Best speech recognition: Chrome on Windows/Mac/Android. iPhone/iPad: Safari (Apple's engine).
